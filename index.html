About us

The Seed of an Idea

fig. 1 — germination
Stage I · Imbibition0 %

Rooted in the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Algiers 1, Hope Buds Club sprouted from one simple conviction: to protect nature, we must first understand it, then share what we learn. From the lab bench to the green spaces of Algiers, we sow curiosity and grow action for a greener, more living planet.

We are students of biology, ecology, chemistry and Earth sciences. Together we turn lectures into fieldwork, and fieldwork into action: biodiversity surveys, sorting and reuse workshops, and talks open to everyone.

Our ambition is to train a generation of young Algerian scientists who understand biodiversity loss and global change, and who act on them with rigour, on campus and across the country.

  • Observe

    Get out in the field, identify, count. A species we do not know is a species we cannot protect.

  • Reduce

    Rethink our waste as a resource by bringing the circular economy into everyday faculty life.

  • Share

    Run national seminars and awareness days so that knowledge reaches beyond the lecture halls.

      Questions

      Good to know

      The essentials before you plant your seed. Another question? Write to us at the address at the bottom of the page.

      Who can join the club?

      Every student at the University of Algiers 1, whatever their field: biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, computer science or any other.

      Do I need experience in ecology?

      No. Curiosity is enough: we learn together in the field, in workshops and at seminars.

      Where and when does the club meet?

      At the Faculty of Sciences, in central Algiers. Registered members receive the date of each meeting by email.

      What happens after I register?

      You immediately receive a confirmation email. The committee then reviews your registration and sends you a second email with our answer, your cell (your first choice when possible) and the date of the next meeting.

      Can I suggest my own project?

      Yes, mention it in the registration form. The Project Development Cell will look at it with you and help turn it into a concrete initiative.

      What do you do with my personal data?

      Your answers are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the club committee can see. We use them only to process your registration and to contact you about the club, and we never share them. They are kept for the 2026–2027 season, then deleted. To see, correct or delete your data, write to hopebuds.club@univ-alger.dz.
